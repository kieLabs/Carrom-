THE RULES OF THE TABLE

How to play.

Learn the basics before taking your seat.

01

Choose your colour

One player pockets white coins; the other pockets black coins. The red queen is the most valuable piece.

02

Aim your striker

Move your striker left or right along your baseline. Drag backwards from the striker to set direction and power, then release.

03

Pocket your coins

Clear all nine coins of your colour. A successful pocket of your own coin earns another turn.

04

Cover the queen

Pocket the red queen together with one of your own coins in the same shot to cover it. If the queen goes in alone, it returns to the centre.

05

Avoid a foul

Do not pocket the striker. A striker foul costs a turn and one of your pocketed coins may be returned to the board.

06

Win the match

Clear all your colour coins and cover the queen. Practice mode is relaxed and has no opponent turn.

REAL-TIME TABLE PHYSICS

Coins collide, rebound from the cushions, slow down with surface friction and drop into pockets. Aim carefully—power is not everything.
